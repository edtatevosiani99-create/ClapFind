# ClapFind

Android app to locate a misplaced phone by clapping or whistling.

- Press ON to begin monitoring.
- Monitoring is intended to continue in the background until OFF is pressed.
- When a sound is detected, the phone should play an alarm, vibrate, and flash the torch when available.
- During the alarm, tap the app screen or press STOP to silence it while keeping monitoring ON.

This is an initial project and must be built and tested on the target Android phone. Sound detection thresholds may need adjustment.
